→ Clinical action: Lower priority

**Q: Why do Medium and Very Low all have identical scores?**
A: They represent specific evidence combinations that occur frequently.
   Medium = predicted + intermediate binding + Class I (n=99)
   Very Low = predicted only (n=1,195)
   This reflects categorical data structure, not model limitations.

**Q: Is the High tier range (98.77-99.99%) meaningful?**
A: For clinical decisions, probably not - all merit high confidence.
   For prioritization and provenance: yes - documents evidence quality.
   Similar to medical evidence grading (Level I/II/III).

**Q: Isn't this just binary classification?**
A: Primary distinction is binary (validated vs predicted), BUT:
   - Context-awareness prevents inappropriate transfer
   - Predicted stratification (23-64%) enables prioritization
   - Transparent provenance documentation
   - Extensible to future data

**Q: How is this better than other tools?**
A: 1. Context-aware (protein-specific validation)
